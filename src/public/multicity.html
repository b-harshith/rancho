        Methodology & sources

        Every headline is labeled as direct, derived or modeled so evidence strength is visible at the point of use.

        DIRECT

        Reported enrollment · all grades

        The sum of source-reported enrollment for schools in the selected audience determines the candidate recommendation and enrollment rank.

        DERIVED

        Grades 2–9 planning audience

        Grades 2–9 is derived only from source-reported school rows. It drives the local demand map, candidate catchments and campus scenarios.

        DIRECT

        Residential inventory

        Project names, locations and known units come from the supplied residential source. Units indicate source-listed marketing reach; occupancy is not inferred.

        MODELED

        Modeled additions & scenarios

        Modeled student additions stay separate from reported evidence. Campus cases use 1%, 2% and 3% of derived Grades 2–9 and round down to fully supportable campuses.

        Recommendation guardrail: source-reported all-grade enrollment sets the market order. Residential depth, partner fit, competition and economics are validation gates—not hidden score adjustments.
