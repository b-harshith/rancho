Before graph analysis

Rankings are based purely on affluence score — a weighted composite of society, hospital, and enterprise office proximity signals. This treats every hex independently .

  • High-affluence but isolated cells rank near the top
  • No awareness of neighborhood network effect
  • Misses clusters that reinforce each other's geographic reach
to
After PageRank analysis

Rankings are network-aware . Cells that sit at the intersection of premium clusters receive a centrality boost proportional to the quality of their neighbors.

  • Strategic Hubs rise — dense connectivity + good affluence
  • Wealth Islands drop — wealthy but isolated, lower reach
  • Context signal becomes contextually amplified by network position

Methodology & Sources

What is observed, what is calculated, what is only a planning scenario, and what can influence a city recommendation.

01

Directly observed

Source values shown without a demand conversion.

MetricSource and coverageUse in recommendation
Source-reported school enrollmentAll-grade enrollment from source-reported school rows; coverage is reported per city and bucket. It remains separate from derived Grade 2–9 and modeled rows.Primary school-demand input in the city recommendation and ranking.
School, board, fee bucket, campus locationSupplied school records, canonical entities, and physical campus grouping.Defines Premium+ scope, partnership targets, and school-location catchments.
Residential project and known unitsSupplied project/listing layer. Missing units remain unavailable.Marketing and operational context; no resident-student or household conversion.
02

Directly calculated

Transparent rollups of observed source values.

MetricFormula and coverageUse in recommendation
Derived reported Premium+ Grade 2–9For each source-reported school row, total enrollment is prorated by the overlap between Grades 2–9 and the supplied grade span, then summed. Example: a Grades 1–12 school contributes 8/12 of reported total enrollment.Local catchment ordering and campus-scenario basis; not the primary city-ranking input.
School and campus countsDistinct canonical school entities and distinct grouped physical campuses.Partnership density and operational reach.
Catchment concentrationReported Premium+ Grade 2–9 in a geography divided by the city reported Premium+ total.Qualifies concentration; never interpreted as home location.
Known residential unitsSum of available units on named project records; records with unknown units stay unknown.Marketing depth qualifier, not a student or household estimate.
03

Planning scenarios

Adjustable operating assumptions; never presented as forecasts.

ScenarioFormulaUse in recommendation
Campus capacity rangeDerived reported Premium+ Grade 2–9 × 1%, 2%, or 3% capture ÷ (200 seats × 80% utilization), rounded down to fully supportable campuses.Planning range only; excluded from city ranking.
Modeled enrollment additionShown separately where a source-reported enrollment value is unavailable and a documented model produced an estimate.Secondary sensitivity only; excluded from the reported headline.
Drive-time catchmentGoogle live-traffic drive-time geometry at 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes around a proposed site. School enrollment remains attached to schools inside the returned geometry, and overlapping catchments must not be added.Site validation only; excluded from the city ranking.
04

Directional indicators

Useful context that cannot establish student residence or conversion.

IndicatorInterpretationUse in recommendation

Residential positioning labelsMapped source project categories. They describe project positioning, not household income.Marketing prioritization only.
Access and routing contextCurrent drive-time geometry around a candidate site when a client-controlled Google key is supplied.Operational qualifier only.
